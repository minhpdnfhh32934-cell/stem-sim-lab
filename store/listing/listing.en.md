## Description

SimLab is a virtual lab for learning physics, chemistry and biology, running right on your
Windows PC.

Drag a slider and watch the simulation respond: projectiles, pendulums, collisions, inclined
planes, springs; atoms, electron configurations, 3D molecules, equation balancing, verified
reactions; mitosis, meiosis, genetics, ecology, enzymes.

Every number in a simulation is computed by tested formulas and numerical methods, never guessed
by an AI. Each simulation has a Science Card with its model, equations, assumptions, confidence
level and sources.

AI Teacher (optional, for people aged 18 or over): ask questions, get hints, and watch the teacher
explain on a whiteboard — handwriting, formulas, circles, underlines, graphs, force diagrams — with
a Vietnamese voice. Use your own API key (Gemini, Claude, Groq) or the Gemma model running on your
PC, offline. AI answers are always labelled "AI-assisted" and have a report button.

Learn by exploring: predict, observe, explain; challenges; step-by-step hints. No leaderboards or
streaks. No accounts, no ads, no usage statistics.

## What's new — 0.8.1

Version 0.8.1:

- A new "Lab bench" interface: pick a lesson on the shelf, set values in the tray, press Run on the bench edge.
- New Physics lessons: Uniform circular motion, Standing waves, Two-source interference, Electric field, DC circuits, Forced oscillation, Harmonics and Fourier series, Kepler's second law.
- Chemistry: Acid–base titration, metal and NaCl crystals in 3D, the reaction energy diagram, molecules filtered by functional group.
- Biology: Prokaryotic and eukaryotic cells (3D and 2D), From cell to DNA.
- The library can show Grade 10, 11 or 12 following the Kết nối tri thức textbooks; the lab is now called Virtual Lab.
- Easier to read and to press: no text below 12 px, buttons at least 40 px, a clear outline when using the keyboard, long names no longer cut off.
- Lessons with repeating motion (interference, standing waves, pendulums, predator–prey…) run until you press Pause; the Science Card says when the energy drifts.
- Many bug fixes, and libraries with known vulnerabilities updated.
