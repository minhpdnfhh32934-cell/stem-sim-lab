## Description

SimLab is a virtual lab for learning physics, chemistry and biology, running right on your
Windows PC.

Drag a slider and watch the simulation respond: projectiles, pendulums, collisions, inclined
planes, springs; atoms, electron configurations, 3D molecules, equation balancing, verified
reactions; mitosis, meiosis, genetics, ecology, enzymes.

Every number in a simulation is computed by tested formulas and numerical methods, never guessed
by an AI. Each simulation has a Science Card with its model, equations, assumptions, confidence
level and sources.

AI Teacher (optional, for people aged 18 or over): ask questions, get hints, and watch the teacher
explain on a whiteboard — handwriting, formulas, circles, underlines, graphs, force diagrams — with
a Vietnamese voice. Use your own API key (Gemini, Claude, Groq) or the Gemma model running on your
PC, offline. AI answers are always labelled "AI-assisted" and have a report button.

Learn by exploring: predict, observe, explain; challenges; step-by-step hints. No leaderboards or
streaks. No accounts, no ads, no usage statistics.
