## 4. How an experiment is done (§11 D1)

Recommended: as the textbook does it, by hand, with the instruments a school has — a digital timer
with photogates, spring balances, a thermometer or temperature sensor, a joulemeter, a manometer, an
oscilloscope, a signal generator, a power supply, meters, a rheostat. Their own buttons and knobs
set them; the remote control desks (four dials, RUN/STOP/RESET, a monitor) go. The results panel and
graphs stay 2D (Phú, 2026-11-02). The titration by hand (Phase B) is the pattern.

## 5. Models (pure, deterministic, tested against their analytic solutions)

v = d/Δt; s = ½gt²; force composition (vector sum) and moments (Σ M = 0); p = p₀ + ρgh; driven damped
oscillator (steady amplitude A(ω), analytic); oscilloscope trace from a tone (f, amplitude, time
base); two-source interference (path difference) and Young's fringes i = λD/a; standing waves
fₙ = n·√(T/μ)/2L; closed-tube resonance L = (2k + 1)λ/4 with v(T) = 331.3·√(1 + t/273.15) m/s;
Coulomb field of point charges; RC circuits; U = E − Ir; Q = mcΔT with the calorimeter's own heat
capacity (heat losses: §11 D9); Q = mλ, Q = mL; pV = const (isothermal), V/T = const (isobaric);
F = BIL·sin α; Faraday's law for a moving magnet and a rotating coil; N = N₀·2^(−t/T½). Values for
the materials (c, λ, L, ρ, μ) from a cited table, never guessed.

  (§4), the remote desks removed. (b) Keep the remote desks with dials for physics.
- **D2 — the wing.** (a) **Recommended:** the plan of §3 (lab by chapter, a dark Waves & Optics
  room, the bay and the range each for what needs their size). (b) One big physics hall for all.
- **D3 — order.** (a) **Recommended:** Physics first (T-1 … T-4), then Chemistry, then Biology. (b)
  The three subjects by turns, grade by grade.
- **D4 — radioactivity.** (a) **Recommended:** a simulated counter whose counts are random as real
  decay is (seeded: the same run gives the same counts), labelled a simulation. (b) Only the smooth
  curve N₀·2^(−t/T½).
- **D5 — geometric optics (grade 9).** (a) **Recommended:** kept as revision, in the optics room.
  (b) Removed from the game.
- **D6 — old saves.** (a) **Recommended:** migrated (to the wing's door, physics settings reset).
  (b) Saves from before Phase T start a new game.
- **D7 — the work not yet committed.** (a) **Recommended:** Phú checks §18.2–§18.4 and S-1 and they
  are committed before T-1 touches the rooms. (b) T-1 builds on top; a checkpoint ref (not on
  `main`) marks today's state first.
- **D8 — the textbook series.** (a) **Recommended:** Kết nối tri thức as the reference (its practicals
  are the program's); (b) another series Phú names.
- **D9 — heat losses in calorimetry.** (a) **Recommended:** modelled (Newton's cooling with a stated

  An ideal calorimeter (no loss).
