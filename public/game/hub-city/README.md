# The city under the hub's glass floor (Phase V-2)

Models and textures from the **Downtown City MegaKit** by Quaternius (Standard, free version),
https://quaternius.itch.io/downtown-city-megakit — CC0 1.0 Universal (public domain dedication),
https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/. Downloaded 2026-11-07 with the owner's permission.

Only the glTF models used are shipped (three prebuilt buildings, the 4-lane street, the
intersection, the sidewalk tile, a 9 × 9 m asphalt patch); their textures were resized from
2048 px to 1024 px and saved as JPEG (the street decals keep PNG: they have alpha). Loaded under
the High and Ultra graphics presets only, with the hub (`src/platform/render/city`).
